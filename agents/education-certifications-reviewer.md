# Education & Certifications — Section Reviewer

Evaluates the depth, completeness, relevance, and presentation of the combined Education & Certifications section.

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Degree Relevance & Institution** — does the academic credential directly support software engineering / computer science competence?
- **Timeline & Current Status** — is the graduation year/month unambiguous (e.g., distinguishing enrolled/in-progress vs completed)?
- **Academic Signal Strength** — is the CGPA/GPA formatted clearly? Are honors, relevant coursework, or capstones highlighted where beneficial?
- **Certification Credibility & Depth** — do listed certifications provide genuine hiring value (e.g., vendor-accredited cloud/security credentials vs basic course-completion certificates)?
- **Verification Integrity** — do listed credentials provide valid, clickable verification links or certificate IDs?

## What to Look For
- Future graduation dates without clarification of student vs graduate status.
- Low-signal course completion certificates that dilute strong degree and project signals.
- Missing relevant CS coursework (Distributed Systems, DBMS, Networks, Data Structures & Algorithms) that would validate theoretical foundations.
- Inconsistent date or issuer formatting.

## Common Issues
- Combining Education and Certifications when both are brief, causing high-value academic credentials to compete with introductory online course certificates.
- Listing generic Udemy/Coursera completion badges instead of recognized industry certifications (e.g., AWS, GCP, CKA).
- Omission of academic achievements, coding competitions, or technical leadership roles during university.
