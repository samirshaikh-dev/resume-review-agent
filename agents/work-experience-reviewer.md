# Work Experience — Section Reviewer

Evaluates the depth, completeness, relevance, and presentation of the Work Experience section.

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Impact vs Tasks** — are bullets results-oriented with clear business/engineering impact, or merely listing responsibilities?
- **Quantification & Scale** — does each bullet provide concrete metrics (latency reductions, RPS/QPS, user count, time saved, data volume)?
- **Ownership Signals** — does the candidate use strong, active ownership verbs ("Architected", "Engineered", "Reduced", "Delivered") rather than passive phrasing ("Assisted with", "Contributed to")?
- **Technical Coherence** — are technology choices and architectural designs justified by the problem context?
- **Timeline & Progression** — are internship/job tenures clear, realistic, and free from misleading date representations?

## What to Look For
- Every bullet should answer the "So What?" question: What tangible improvement did the engineering work bring?
- Absence of baseline metrics when percentages are claimed (e.g., "Reduced latency by 30%" — from what baseline to what target?).
- Misalignment between listed tenure (e.g., 1-month intern) and claimed scope ("Solo end-to-end delivery of entire production platform").
- Missing context regarding testing, CI/CD pipelines, code review, and cross-functional team collaboration.
- Technologies mentioned without context (e.g., listing a library without explaining why it was required).

## Common Issues
- Bullets reading like job descriptions or task summaries rather than high-impact accomplishments.
- Percentages without absolute benchmarks or operational scale context.
- Zero mention of automated test suites, error rates, or system reliability metrics.
- Unsubstantiated claims of "production-ready" or "sub-100ms" without profiling or load testing evidence.
