# Certifications — Section Reviewer

Evaluates the depth, completeness, relevance, and presentation of the Certifications section.

## Evaluation Criteria
- **Relevance** — are the certifications relevant to software engineering and full stack development?
- **Credibility** — are they issued by reputable platforms (e.g., Udemy, AWS, Coursera, Google)?
- **Completeness** — do they include credential IDs or verification links?
- **Recency** — are the dates clear and up to date?

## What to Look For
- Missing credential links or verification IDs.
- Vague or generic certificates that do not add technical value (e.g., soft skills certificates that are better demonstrated elsewhere).
- Outdated or expired certifications.

## Common Issues
- Listing certifications without clickable credentials or ID verification.
- Overloading the section with introductory courses that detract from advanced qualifications.
- Inconsistent date formats or missing issuer details.
