# Summary — Probing Questions

This file contains targeted interview questions based on the candidate's professional **Summary**.

## Technical Depth & Full-Stack Ownership
1. You describe yourself as a Full Stack Engineer owning features end-to-end. Can you walk through a production feature you delivered from database schema design (PostgreSQL/MongoDB) up through the Next.js/React UI? What architectural trade-offs did you make along the way?
2. How do you balance performance optimizations between the frontend (e.g., SSR, streaming, bundle reduction) and backend (e.g., connection pooling, indexing, caching layers)?

## Integrations & Security
1. You highlight experience with role-based access control (RBAC) and payment gateway integrations. How do you ensure secure, idempotent transactions and prevent authorization bypasses across full-stack architectures?

## Collaboration & Agile Process
1. How do you collaborate with product managers, designers, and QA engineers in Agile sprint cycles when requirements shift or technical debt threatens delivery deadlines?
2. When introducing new libraries, databases, or third-party services into an existing codebase, what evaluation criteria do you use to justify the decision?
